# The smash's recordings

Every file here is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution is
required. The makers are named anyway, with thanks.

Each was cut from the source named below, mixed to mono, trimmed of leading
silence, given a 60 ms fade out, normalised to -1 dBFS and encoded as MP3
(44.1 kHz, LAME VBR quality 6) with ffmpeg. `boom.mp3` was also low-passed at
420 Hz with a bass lift, to keep only its weight. About 100 KB together.

MP3, not Ogg or WebM Opus: every browser the app runs in decodes it,
iOS Safari included.
