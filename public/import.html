ONOSENDAI · BLOCK BUILDS

Import a build

Works with .schem, .litematic and .nbt files exported from Minecraft.

OPEN THE IMPORTER

Everything stays on your device. Your file is read in your browser and is never uploaded. Nothing goes out until you hide or publish the build yourself.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. EXPORT YOUR BUILDWith WorldEdit: select it, //copy, then //schem save. With Litematica: save a schematic. With a structure block: save a structure (up to 48 blocks a side).
  2. DROP THE FILEOpen the importer and drop the file in, or tap to pick it. On a phone, pick it from your files.
  3. LOOK IT OVERYour build turns in the preview. A Litematica file with several regions shows each one: leave out what you do not want.
  4. CHOOSE THE COLOURSEach kind of block gets one of our 256 colours. Tap a colour to change it. MERGE SIMILAR gives blocks of one kind one colour, which makes the build smaller and smoother.
  5. CHOOSE THE SIZE1, 2 or 4 gibsons a block. The importer shows the real size and the height it hides at.
  6. SAVE IT TO YOUR WORKSHOPIt lands in your IMPORTS folder as a shard of yours. A big build is cut into pieces by place; each piece is signed once when you hide it. PUBLISH is there and starts off.
  7. PLACE ITPLACE opens BUILD with your build on the cursor. Hide it anywhere in cyberspace.

WHAT COMES ACROSS

  • Shapes: whole blocks, and slabs, stairs, fences, walls, panes, doors and thin layers drawn simply.
  • Glass, ice and water: see-through, each in a piece of its own. You can walk through water.
  • Plants, torches, lanterns and other small things are left out unless you switch them on.
  • Colours are ours, picked by eye against our palette. No textures come across, and none are used.
  • Chest contents, sign text, mobs and other entities are not part of a build and are left out.

QUESTIONS AND PROBLEMS
